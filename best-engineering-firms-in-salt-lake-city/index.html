Every firm in this ranking is scored on the same six criteria, in this order:

  2. Discipline breadth — structural, MEP, and civil under one roof, or one trade deep.
  3. Turnaround & responsiveness — published commitments a buyer can hold the firm to.
  4. Project-type fit — the work this market is actually buying.
  5. Capacity model — a bench that absorbs your project, or a first-come queue.
  6. Buyer proof — published evidence that buyers win with this firm.

This is one of America’s youngest, fastest-growing metros: sustained multifamily and industrial and warehouse building along the I-15 corridor, downtown investment momentum, “Silicon Slopes” tech campuses and data-center volume pushing south toward Lehi and Utah County, and high-elevation work — with real snow loads — spilling toward the mountain markets around Park City. A buyer juggling a corridor warehouse, a multifamily set, and a tech fit-out does not need three firms; it needs one department with reserved partner capacity for drafting firms, GCs, modular builders, architects, and owners. Apex is a 100% service-disabled veteran-owned small business (SDVOSB), founded and led by USAF veteran Jeremy Mills — veteran-owned, with licensed PEs stamping drawings in days, not months, at the best price, with client reviews published on its own site from architects, contractors, and developers.
